# Website Design Brief — Prompt for Claude Design

Use: copy everything below the line into Claude Design and attach the Elite Academy logo files (SVG or high-resolution PNG, plus any existing brand colours).

---

Design a website template for **Elite Academy**, a martial arts academy in Kuwait with two branches. I have attached our logo. Build the colour palette from the logo, and keep the logo's proportions and clear space intact.

## Who we are

- **Name:** Elite Academy (Elite Martial Arts Academy), founded in 2015, Kuwait. Website: elitemaacademy.com
- **Tagline:** People · Progress · A Stronger You
- **Vision:** We build better humans. Elite Academy contributes to a better society through martial arts: building character, discipline and responsibility for ourselves and others.
- **Our five principles:**
  1. **Safety First.** If it's not safe, we don't do it. Safe training, safe places, safe treatment of children.
  2. **Discipline.** Show up. Repeat. Improve.
  3. **Accountability.** Own your choices. Get back up. Learn from mistakes.
  4. **Tough Love.** Be the example. Hold the standard. Firm, fair and caring.
  5. **Team First.** Respect the individual. Protect the group.
- **Supporting lines we use:** "Build the warrior spirit." "Different skills. Same purpose. Stronger together." "Discipline today, brighter tomorrow."

## Our two branches (they are different products and must look distinct but belong to one brand)

**Elite Promenade, Promenade Mall, Hawalli: "The Complete Fighter Program"**
- Kajukenbo · Kickboxing · Jiu-Jitsu. Ages 5–12 train both striking and grappling, alternating through the week; ages 13+ continue into Jiu-Jitsu.
- 85 KD/month, all disciplines included. Trial class 10 KD. Open daily except Thursday and Friday.
- Audience: families at the mall who weren't looking for a martial arts school. Message: a multi-year path, not a single class.

**Elite Bedaya, Sabah Al Salem: "Choose Your Discipline"**
- Taekwondo and Kyokushin Karate (Sat / Mon / Wed), Kickboxing and Kajukenbo (Sun / Tue / Thu). Boys 6–13 and adults, plus a **dedicated ladies' class for girls 6+ and adult women**, which is rare in Kuwait.
- Kids 65 KD/month, adults 55 KD/month. Trial 10 KD. Open every day except Friday.
- Audience: the local community and word of mouth. Message: choice, flexibility and value. It must never read as "the cheaper branch."

**Six specialized instructors**, each an expert in their own discipline. Instructor profiles show rank, years of training and competition background.

**Belt progression** is central: visible progress (belts, stripes, levels) is what keeps families with us. The Kajukenbo path is White → Yellow → Purple → Blue → Green → Brown → Brown/Black stripe → Black.

## Who the website is for

1. **Parents** (the main decision-makers) looking for a safe, disciplined, caring place for their children. They need trust: safety, qualified instructors, clear prices, and visible progress.
2. **Adults**, including **women** looking for a comfortable, respectful ladies' class.
3. **Current members and parents** checking the schedule, events, gallery, shop and their portal.

## Feeling and visual direction

- The site should feel **disciplined, warm and trustworthy**: a respected dojo that is also a welcoming family place. Think of the respect of the bow, clean mats and a proud belt ceremony.
- **Avoid** aggressive fight-night or MMA-cage imagery, blood, dark grunge textures, and generic gym-template looks. Also avoid generic corporate stock-photo layouts.
- Use real training photography (children and adults training, belt ceremonies, seminars, camps) as the main visual material. Design image slots so that our own photos drop in. Use tasteful placeholders now.
- Consider using the **belt colours as a quiet design system**, for example for program levels, progress indicators or section markers, without turning the site into a rainbow.
- Include a subtle nod to our playbook illustrations (hand-drawn line figures in gis), for example in empty states or the principles section, if it fits the direction.

## Language and layout requirements

- **Fully bilingual: Arabic and English.** Every layout must work in **right-to-left Arabic** and left-to-right English, with a language switch in the header. Show key screens in both directions.
- Choose typefaces with strong Arabic and Latin families that pair well (for example IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Tajawal, Cairo, Noto Kufi Arabic, or better if you find it). Headings need character; body text needs to be very readable on phones.
- **Mobile first**, since most parents will visit from WhatsApp links and Instagram on their phones. Large tap targets and fast-loading pages.
- Accessible contrast and focus states throughout.

## The site will be managed by a CMS, so design it as reusable blocks

Our staff will build and edit pages from blocks. Please design a consistent block library, each block in Arabic and English and in desktop and mobile:

- Hero (image or video, headline, two buttons: "Book a trial" and "See schedule")
- Branch comparison (Promenade vs Bedaya, side by side)
- Program card and program grid (discipline, ages, days, price, belt path)
- Class schedule / timetable (weekly grid, filter by branch, program and age; readable on a phone)
- Instructor card and instructor profile
- Our principles / values block
- Belt progression block (the path from White to Black)
- Testimonial / parent quote
- Events list and event card (seminars, camps, tournaments, belt tests, showcases)
- **Photo gallery:** album grid, album page and a full-screen photo viewer with swipe
- News / announcement card
- Promotion banner with an end date (for example "Free trial until 31 August")
- FAQ accordion
- Call-to-action strip
- Location block (map placeholder, address, opening days, parking and mall directions)
- Footer (branches, contact, WhatsApp, Instagram, language switch)

## Pages to design (desktop and mobile)

1. **Home.** Our vision, the two branches, programs, why Elite (complete fighter training, ladies' class, specialized instructors, safety), belt path, upcoming events, latest gallery album, testimonials, and book a trial.
2. **Branch page** (show Promenade, and note how Bedaya would differ).
3. **Program page** (for example Kajukenbo: who it's for, ages, schedule, price, belt path, instructor).
4. **Schedule.**
5. **Events** list and one **event page.**
6. **Gallery**: albums and a single album with the photo viewer.
7. **Online shop**: product list (gloves, uniforms, shin guards, head guards, rash guards, T-shirts), product page with a **size selector and size guide** (gloves in oz, uniforms in cm, guards XS–XL), cart, and checkout with **KNET and Visa/MC** and "pick up at branch" or delivery. Prices in Kuwaiti Dinar with 3 decimals (for example 10.000 KD).
8. **Book a trial**: only **name and phone** plus branch and program, and a friendly confirmation screen ("Our instructor will confirm your class time").
9. **About us**: story since 2015, vision, five principles and instructors.
10. **Contact.**
11. **Member portal sign-in** (by mobile number and one-time code), with one example of the parent's view: each child's next class, membership expiry and current belt with progress to the next.

## Chat assistant

Every page has a floating chat button for **SAQR**, our AI assistant, which answers questions about programs, schedules and prices and books trials. Design the closed button and the open chat panel on desktop and mobile, in Arabic and English. It should feel like part of the brand, with a warm greeting in our tone. Example: "Hi! Thanks for reaching out to Elite Academy. Happy to help. Are you asking about our Hawalli or Sabah Al Salem branch?"

## Tone of the words

Warm, confident and direct, like a trusted coach rather than a call centre. Keep text short and use real content from this brief, not lorem ipsum. Say "Our instructor will assess…", not "You need approval…". Say "Let's find the right fit for your child", not "You have to pick a class". Never describe Promenade as "premium" in a way that makes Bedaya sound lesser.

## What I want back

1. **Two or three distinct design directions** for the Home page (desktop and mobile), each with a short explanation of the idea. I will choose one.
2. For the chosen direction: all pages listed above, in desktop and mobile, with key screens also in Arabic (RTL).
3. A **component sheet** of all CMS blocks and their states (for example hover, active, empty gallery, sold-out size, form error).
4. **Design tokens:** colour palette (derived from our logo, with light and dark values if you propose a dark mode), typography scale for Arabic and English, spacing, corner radius, and shadows. My developer will build the site from them.
