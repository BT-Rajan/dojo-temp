The Prostho Head Manager app is a departmental operations tool that helps the Head of Prosthodontics efficiently:

Assign doctors to regular and examination clinics.

Track who’s on leave or has permission.

Distribute emergency walk-ins fairly.

Maintain daily coverage visibility.

Streamline recurring weekly schedules.

Ensure accountability and balance in doctor workload.

👩‍⚕️ Doctor Management
Add, edit, or delete doctors.

Each doctor profile includes:

Name

Title

Civil ID

File number

Phone number

Doctors can be marked automatically as on leave or available based on stored leave dates.

🗓️ Clinic Assignment (Weekly)
Add, edit, or delete regular clinics.

Assign a doctor to one or more clinics.

For each clinic assignment:

Select one or more weekdays (Sunday–Thursday).

The app repeats these weekly unless changed.

Doctors on leave are excluded automatically from clinic duties during their leave period.

🚨 Emergency Walk-In Management
Pressing the “Assign Emergency” button:

Suggests the next eligible doctor.

Skips those on leave or with permission.

Rotates fairly based on how many times each doctor has been assigned.

Ensures fair emergency distribution throughout the month.

🌴 Leave Management
From a doctor’s profile:

Add leave with start and end dates.

App uses this info to:

Block clinic and emergency assignments.

Reinstate doctor automatically once leave ends.

Includes a Leave Summary View to track:

All current and upcoming leaves in one place.

🕐 Permission Tracking
Doctors can request:

Start-of-Day permission (e.g. 7:30–9:30)

End-of-Day permission (e.g. 12:30–2:30)

App allows up to 4 permissions/month per doctor.

Automatically blocks the doctor during those times.

Includes a Permission History View:

View all permissions by month.

Option to Clear All Permissions monthly.

🧪 Examination Clinic Scheduling
Separate view to assign doctors to specific time slots (e.g., 10:00–11:00) per weekday.

Allows multiple doctors per time slot.

Saved schedules are repeated weekly.

Doctors on leave are auto-removed from the day’s examination slots.

📅 Daily View
Shows which clinics are covered today and by whom.

Indicates:

Doctors on leave

Doctors with permissions

Any clinic without coverage (highlighted)

One-tap emergency assignment for today’s coverage.

🔁 Automation Features
All clinic assignments, examination schedules, and emergency rotations auto-repeat weekly unless updated.

Leave and permissions override availability automatically.

Designed to minimize manual planning and last-minute gaps.
